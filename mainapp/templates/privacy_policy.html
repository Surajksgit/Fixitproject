Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FIXIT. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely and responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when you use our website and platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name, contact phone number, email address, service location, and service preferences when you book an appointment or register an account. We also collect usage diagnostics to improve platform response times and service reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is utilized solely to match you with qualified, background-checked technicians, coordinate timely arrival, process secure digital payments, and provide post-service warranty support. We never sell your personal data to third-party brokers.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All sensitive communications, passwords, and payment transactions are secured using standard SSL encryption and processed through certified payment gateways. Access to your personal data is restricted strictly to verified technicians assigned to your active service ticket.

5. Your Rights & Control

You have full rights to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your account and personal history at any time by contacting our support team at support@fixit.com.
