Our Purpose & Story

Making Home Maintenance Simple & Trustworthy

We bridge the gap between discerning homeowners and certified, background-checked craftsmen. Reliable quality, upfront pricing, and absolute peace of mind.

Our Foundation

Built to Eliminate Friction in Home Services

Finding a reliable tradesperson used to mean unpredictable pricing, unverified credentials, and missed appointments. FIXIT was founded with a singular vision: to bring standardized quality, transparent rates, and vetted experts to every household.

Every specialist on our network is personally vetted, skill-benchmarked, and backed by our comprehensive $10,000 property protection policy and 30-day rework guarantee.
